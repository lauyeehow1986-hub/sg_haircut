## Methodology & Honest Caveats
1. **100% Spatial Join Across All `5,409` Pins (`96021 + 96022 Hair/Scalp + OSM/Verified + Audited`):**
   - Every mapped pin (`3,927` ACRA SSIC `96021` + `713` clean Hair/Barber/Scalp UENs from ACRA SSIC `96022` + `514` OpenStreetMap/Verified 2026 storefronts [`491` OSM + `23` directory-verified] + `255` Audited = `5,409`) is joined to URA Master Plan `2019` **55 Planning Areas** (`2104`) and **332 Subzones** (`1749`). Coastal edge pins outside polygon boundaries are snapped to their nearest Planning Area / Subzone centroid.
2. **Official HDB vs. Non-HDB Premises Classification (`2,601` vs. `2,808`):**
   - **ACRA pins (`4,640` total, `2,458` HDB)** match `(blk_no, normalized_street)` against Data.gov.sg **HDB Property Information** (`d_17f5382f26140b1fdae0ba2ef6239d2f`, `13,392` blocks) or SLA OneMap buildings containing `"HDB"`.
   - **OSM/Verified (`514` total, `119` HDB) & Audited (`255` total, `24` HDB) pins** match verified HDB postcodes, `(blk_no, normalized_street)`, or `<= 25m` (`<= 28m` for audited) proximity to an HDB block when not matching known shopping mall names (`MALL_RX`).
   - *Caveat:* An ACRA HDB match confirms the registered address sits inside an official HDB block (e.g., void-deck/podium shophouse or a registered residential/home-based address), not necessarily walk-in commercial zoning for every home-based UEN.
3. **`Spec./10k Youth` & Why Plain-Name Salons / `SSIC 96022` / BTOs Needed Rework:**
   - **Denominator:** Resident children aged `0–14` (`Total_0_4 + Total_5_9 + Total_10_14`) from Data.gov.sg / SingStat Census `2020` (`d_d95ae740c0f8961a0b10435836660ce0`).
   - **Numerator (`counts.spec`):** On **ACRA (`4,640`)** and **OSM/Verified (`514`)**, outlets not tagged as barbers are tagged `spec = True` if their business name matches keywords (`studio`, `atelier`, `curl`, `afro`, `coily`, `hijab`, `muslimah`, `kid/kids/baby/child/junior/toddler`, `scalp/tricholog`, `korean/japanese/shiseido/keratin/rebond/perm/bridal/bespoke/director/boutique`), plus manual audits where verified (e.g. *Salon Shan* `53419715M` at `224A Sumang Ln S(821224)` reclassified `spec=True`, and *Cashin Courier* at `S(822102)` reclassified to *The New Cashin EFFCUTZ* barber). On **Audited (`255`)**, `spec = True` if tier is `T3`/`T4`, `hijab_private == True`, or `biotype_specs` lists `curly`/`coily`/`afro`.
   - *Example (Punggol):* `22` specialist-tagged pins out of `83` total pins (`46` HDB) ÷ `42,900` aged `0–14` (`16,160` aged `0–4`) × `10,000` = **`5.1` per 10k youth**.
   - *Key Caveats on `Spec./10k Youth`:*
     - **Why `224A Sumang Ln` (*Salon Shan*) was initially misclassified as `spec=False`:** `SALON SHAN` (`53419715M`) was already present in our `96021` pin file at `S(821224)`, but its registered UEN name did not contain `"studio/scalp/treatment"`, causing the regex to count it as a plain salon and understate on-site treatment competition at `224A`.
     - **Why `SSIC 96022` & BTO Storefronts Were Added:** `3,927` `SSIC 96021` pins omitted `713` physical hair/barber/scalp outlets registered under `SSIC 96022` (e.g., *Hair Tec.* at `639 Punggol Dr`, *Powercuts* at `115B Alkaff`, *Minna* at `Plantation Plaza`), while volunteer OSM lagged `2024–2026` BTO/mall shops (*Kataomo* `653A`/`507A`, *Hasami Studio* `235B`, *83 Hair Avenue* `218`, *Express Barber Lab* `339A`). Generic `"Studio"` names and un-deduplicated dual ACRA+OSM pins still require checking exact postcodes before bidding.
4. **ACRA Headquarters vs. Multi-Outlet Storefronts:**
   - Multi-outlet express chains (*kcuts*, *QB House*, *Clippers*, *Snip Avenue*) frequently register a single corporate HQ UEN on ACRA rather than separate UENs per mall kiosk, whereas independent neighbourhood salons/barbers typically register physical shop postcodes. That is why all `5,409` pins are presented alongside separate `ACRA` (`4,640`), `OSM/Verified` (`514`), `Audited` (`255`), and `HDB` (`2,601`) filters.
5. **Census `2020` Subzone Baseline vs. `2026` Regional Updates:**
   - Planning Area & Subzone age/ethnic (`d_d95ae740...`, `d_e7ae9017...`) and household-income (`d_4e150bbd...`) figures use the latest Data.gov.sg Census `2020` release and therefore predate post-`2021` BTO completion waves in **Tengah** and **Punggol North**; macro `2020 → 2026` population and `65+` aging trajectories are updated at the Planning Region level via live **SingStat MCP `M810771`** (`2019–2026`) and **`M810091`** (`2025` TFR `0.87`).
6. **Composite Gap Score Indices (`senior_gap_score` & `youth_gap_score`):**
   - **`senior_gap_score`:** Combines `pct_65_plus` (`45%` weight, capped at `26%`), `log10(pop_65_plus)` (`30%` weight), and low-income households `<S$4,000/mo` (`25%` weight, defaulted to `34%` at subzone level where Census income is unavailable), discounted by supply relief `1 / (1 + total_per_10k_seniors/65 + hdb_per_10k_seniors/25)`.
   - **`youth_gap_score`:** Combines `pct_0_14` (`58%` weight, capped at `25%`) and `log10(pop_0_4 + 1)` (`42%` weight), discounted by `1 / (1 + total_per_10k_youth/45 + spec_per_10k_youth/5)`.
7. **Google Reviews, Two-Tier Audited Confidence (`102` vs. `153`) & Places API Cost Constraint:**
   - **Google Places API was NOT used** to verify or bulk-fetch star ratings, review counts, or permanent closures across all `5,409` pins due to API cost (`Places API (New) Text Search Enterprise` charges `US$35 / 1,000` calls after a `1,000`/month free cap, or `~US$151–$186` across the `5,307` unrated pins; bulk headless scraping is similarly excluded due to rate/anti-bot gates).
   - Instead, the original `102` *Outlet-Verified Review+Menu* outlets retain individually verified Google star ratings and review counts inline; the `153` newly added *Chain-Standard Menu* branches carry verified official chain menus and OneMap coordinates with blank ratings/reviews (zero fabricated review counts); and all `5,409` pins provide direct 1-tap **Google Maps & Reviews** search links (`googleReviewsUrl`) in the browser.
8. **PubMed Hair-Growth Simulation, Biological Gaps & Establishment-Driver Caveats:**
   - **No sex-based linear velocity gap:** Clinical phototrichogram studies (`PMID: 11531795`, `37667034`, `24185569`) show zero statistically significant linear growth-velocity (`µm/day`) difference between healthy males and females; the modeled `~2.2×` cut-count gap (`7.90` vs `3.57/yr`) separates **biology** (male AGA `PMID: 11063167`, postpartum TE `PMID: 23682615`) from **style-length tolerance** and cultural practice (`Kesh`, hijab trims).
   - **Ethnic velocity proxy gap:** PubMed contains clinical phototrichogram velocity benchmarks for East Asian (`411 µm/day`, `PMID: 16187948`, `25065943`), Caucasian (`367 µm/day`), and African (`256–280 µm/day`) hair, plus pediatric linear growth (`PMID: 37667034`) and SE Asian hair diameter (`PMID: 29543618`), but **no standalone phototrichogram `µm/day` trial specifically on Singapore Malays or Singapore Indians**. Both are explicitly bracketed via sensitivity ranges.
   - **No global/regional haircut benchmarking via Google Data Commons (`datacommons-mcp`):** Live queries against Google Data Commons across `country/SGP`, `country/KOR`, `country/JPN`, `country/MYS`, `country/GBR`, and `country/USA` confirm Data Commons verifies our official population denominator (`Count_Person` for Singapore = `6,208,523` in `2026` via SingStat), **but contains zero annual haircut-visit frequency (`cuts/yr`) variables and zero disaggregated `NAICS 81211`, `NACE S96.02`, or `COICOP 12.1.1` salon sub-industry tables** (US BLS establishments stop at 2-digit `NAICS 81` *"Other services"*). Cross-country haircut frequency therefore cannot be validated through Google Data Commons.
   - **Why establishments grew (`8,453 → 11,241`):** SingStat `M601481` covers combined **SSIC `9602`** (hairdressing + beauty/spas) and shows **`-S$104.1M` sector Gross Operating Surplus in `2024`** because opex rose `+75.7%`. Cross-referencing Data.gov.sg ACRA `96021` (`3,933` active) vs `96022` (`7,155` active) indicates growth is driven by **specialised beauty/scalp/head-spa services** (`76%–84%` non-cut revenue) and **niche barber (`3.0% → 8.1%`) / solo studio fragmentation** amid high churn (`72%–75%` cumulative exits), not basic haircut profits.
