# Singapore Haircut Econometrics & Spatio-Temporal Salon Dashboard

Live GitHub Pages site: **https://lauyeehow1986-hub.github.io/sg_haircut/**

## What Is Included
- **`index.html`** (and synced copy **`webgis/sg_hairstyle_demographics_explorer.html`**) — 4-tab empirical dashboard covering:
  1. **Yearly Trend in Singapore (`2016–2026`):** Official **SingStat SSIC 9602** Services Survey operating revenue (`S$2,173.8M` in `2019` → `S$3,381.1M` in `2024`, `+55.5%`) and establishment count (`8,453` → `11,241`, `+33.0%`), paired with **Population in Brief (`2016–2026`)** mid-year population (`5.61M` → `5.45M` → `6.21M`), Resident `65+` aging (`12.4%` → `19.5%`), `HES 2023` (`S$5,931/mo`), and an interactive annual haircut demand sensitivity curve.
  2. **Cost at Different Places & Outlet Count Reconciliation (Salons + Barbers):** Reconciles our **`102`** price-audited sample outlets (`S$5.80–S$280` across `T1–T4`) against **SLA OneMap** (`0` retail themes out of `166`), **OpenStreetMap Overpass** (`491` shopfronts for `hairdresser|barber`), **Google Maps directory totals** (`2,819` = `1,981` salons + `838` barbers), **ACRA SSIC `96021`** (`3,933` active entities, `3,927` geocoded), and **SingStat SSIC `9602`** (`11,241` combined establishments).
  3. **Annual Money Spent & SingStat `CMIO` / Age Context:** Transparent **Visits/Year × Audited Tier Ticket + Add-Ons** calculator with zero fabricated biological/racial cadence multipliers, paired with **SingStat 2026 Resident `CMIO` & Age** context (`4.23M` residents) and verified Singapore cultural service rules (Muslimah hijab-private cabins and Sikh Sacred *Kesh* `S$0` haircut fees; `Census 2020`: `12,051` aged `15+`).
  4. **Islandwide WebGIS & Population Gaps (`4,520` Pins + Official HDB + SingStat/Census):** Interactive **OpenStreetMap** (`© OpenStreetMap contributors`, with SLA OneMap layer switcher, `🇸🇬 Center`, `📍 My GPS`) map integrated directly in Tab 4 combining **all `4,520` pins** (`3,927` ACRA SSIC 96021 + `491` OSM + `102` Audited), classified into **`2,162` Official HDB (`47.8%`)** vs. **`2,358` Non-HDB (`52.2%`)** via Data.gov.sg **HDB Property Information** (`d_17f5382f26140b1fdae0ba2ef6239d2f`) and SLA OneMap buildings, overlaid onto **55 URA Planning Areas** & **332 Subzones**, Census 2020 Age/Sex (`d_d95ae740c0f8961a0b10435836660ce0`) & HH Income (`d_4e150bbd35c3145ea43019212c7b46c2`), and **SingStat MCP `M810771` (`2020–2026`)** / **`M810091` (`0.87` TFR)** to highlight aging-town HDB/barber deficits and young-family (`0–4` & `0–14`) specialist salon deficits. Progressive sidebar renders **all matching pins (`4,520` max)** with inline Google ratings on the `102` audited outlets and 1-tap `Reviews ↗` links on all `4,520`.
- **`webgis/sg_haircut_gis_explorer.html`** — Synced standalone full-screen WebGIS page containing the same `4,520` pins, HDB vs. Non-HDB filter, PA/Subzone choropleth, and clickable Service Gap Explorer tables.
