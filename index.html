Post-2021 Demographic Rebound

Total population dropped from 5.70M (2019) to 5.45M (2021) during COVID border restrictions before climbing +13.9% to 6.21M (June 2026), directly lifting salon & barber footfall.

Super-Aged Shift (12.4% → 19.5% 65+)

Residents aged 65+ rose every year from 2016 (12.4%) to 2026 (19.5%; median age 43.6y), expanding demand for accessible HDB void-deck salons & traditional neighbourhood barbers.

Methodological Note: Why There Are No Biological or Racial Cadence Multipliers Here

SingStat publishes resident demographics by CMIO (`Chinese 73.9% · Malay 13.5% · Indian 9.0% · Others 3.6%`), Sex & Age (Table M810011 / Population in Brief 2026) and aggregate SSIC 9602 receipts, not per-capita haircut spend by race. Trichology papers on curly-hair biomechanics also do not predict annual `S$` spend.
